About

Understanding systems as structures, not just stories.

Our focus

Systems Unpacked is a technical publication for people who work on or within complex systems—software architects, platform teams, product leaders, operators, analysts, and executives who need clarity about what is really driving outcomes.

We focus on architecture, trade-offs, incentives, operational constraints, and the feedback loops that shape performance. We break complex systems into readable, evidence-driven explanations without reducing them to abstractions.

What we cover

  • System design and architecture
  • Operational risk and resilience
  • Platform scaling and dependencies
  • Organizational behavior and incentives
  • Market, product, and policy complexity
